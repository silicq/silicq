### Hi, I'm Alex 👋

I study in Norway, and Norwegian isn't my first language – so I'm learning it the slow way, one word at a time.

To make that easier I built **[Ordkort](https://ordkort.com)**: free flashcards in the browser. You pick a topic and the words are already there, with their forms and an example sentence. No account, no ads, and the code is open.

- 🃏 **[ordkort](https://github.com/silicq/ordkort)** – vanilla JS, Cloudflare Workers + D1, Anki decks in and out
- 🇳🇴 [Norwegian words by topic](https://ordkort.com/en/norwegian/) in English, Polish, Ukrainian, Russian and Arabic
- 📫 hei@ordkort.com

Feedback, issues and "this could be better" are always welcome.
